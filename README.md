PolarNexus

AI-Powered Polar Knowledge, Outreach & Digital Twin Platform

PolarNexus is an integrated platform designed to strengthen India’s polar research ecosystem by combining scientific knowledge management, AI-powered research assistance, public outreach, and Antarctic station intelligence into one unified ecosystem.

Key Features
Polar Research Repository

Centralized management and discovery of:

Expedition reports
Scientific datasets
Research publications
Institutional records
Photographs and videos
AI Semantic Search

Enables researchers to search scientific resources using natural-language queries and understand context rather than relying only on exact keywords.

RAG-Based Research Assistant

Allows users to ask questions about polar research documents and receive context-aware answers based on relevant scientific sources.

AI Summarization

Automatically generates concise summaries of:

Research papers
Expedition reports
Scientific documents
Institutional records
Content Generation & Outreach

Converts scientific information into:

Educational articles
Public-friendly content
Website content
Social-media content
Awareness material
Citizen Science

Allows students and the public to participate in selected polar observation, awareness, and science-outreach activities.

Antarctic Digital Twin

Provides an integrated digital representation of Maitri and Bharati stations, covering:

Infrastructure
Energy
Environmental conditions
Logistics and inventory
Assets and equipment
Station health
Predictive Intelligence

AI/ML capabilities support:

Predictive maintenance
Resource consumption forecasting
Anomaly detection
Risk scoring
Early warnings
Operational recommendations
Human-in-the-Loop

The platform does not replace scientists or station personnel. AI analyses data, identifies patterns, predicts risks, and provides recommendations, while humans make the final operational decisions.

Frontend: Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS
Digital Twin: Three.js, React Three Fiber
Backend: Node.js, Express
AI/ML: Python, FastAPI, Scikit-learn, XGBoost
RAG: LLM, Embeddings, pgvector
Database: PostgreSQL
Search: OpenSearch / Elasticsearch
Real-time: WebSocket / Socket.IO
IoT: MQTT
Visualization: ECharts
Maps: Leaflet / Mapbox
Storage: MinIO / S3-compatible storage
Authentication: JWT + RBAC
Deployment: Docker

Data Strategy

The prototype can use publicly available Antarctic datasets along with clearly labelled synthetic operational data for demonstrating station telemetry, energy consumption, inventory, maintenance, and logistics scenarios.

For future deployment, authorized station data can be integrated through existing communication and data infrastructure.

Project Vision

PolarNexus aims to create a unified digital ecosystem where polar research, scientific knowledge, AI, public outreach, and Antarctic station intelligence work together to improve research accessibility, collaboration, operational awareness, and data-driven decision-making.
